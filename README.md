
##Project Overview

This project simulates a **corporate-grade, end-to-end data analytics workflow**, demonstrating the ability to transform raw customer shopping data into **actionable business intelligence**.

The analysis focuses on understanding **customer purchasing behavior, spending patterns, and product performance** to support data-driven decision-making. The workflow covers the complete analytics lifecycle — from data preparation and exploratory analysis to SQL-based insights, interactive dashboards, and business-focused reporting.

Using a structured dataset containing **customer demographics, transaction details, purchase frequency, product categories, and spending amounts**, the project delivers meaningful insights through analytical modeling, visualization, and stakeholder-ready documentation.

---

##Project Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, and shopping frequency
* Identify high-value customers and top-performing product categories
* Understand trends across demographics and customer segments
* Present insights through interactive dashboards and clear business reports

---

##Dataset

* **Source:** Customer Shopping Behavior dataset (CSV)
* **Key attributes include:** customer demographics, transaction history, purchase frequency, product categories, and spending metrics

---

##Tools & Technologies Used

* **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Jupyter Notebook)** for data cleaning, transformation, modeling, and exploratory analysis
* **SQL (MySQL)** for simulating business transactions and extracting customer-level and category-level insights
* **Power BI** for building interactive dashboards, KPI tracking, and trend visualization
* **Excel** for initial data review and validation
* **PowerPoint & PDF** for business reporting, documentation, and insight presentation

---

##Key Analysis Performed

* Customer segmentation based on spending and purchasing behavior
* Evaluation of shopping frequency and average order value
* Category-wise revenue contribution and customer distribution
* Identification of high-value, loyal, and repeat customers

---

##Deliverables

* Python analysis notebook (`.ipynb`)
* SQL analysis scripts (`.sql`)
* Interactive Power BI dashboard (`.pbix`)
* Business presentation (`.pptx`)
* Supporting project documentation (`.pdf`)

---

##Business Impact

The insights generated from this project can support businesses in:

* Improving targeted and personalized marketing strategies
* Optimizing product mix and category performance
* Enhancing customer retention and loyalty initiatives
* Driving revenue growth through data-driven decision-making
